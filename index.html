Questions, answered.

Does LeadPilot replace my HVAC staff?

No. It handles the initial intake and passes the opportunity to your team. Your staff remains responsible for diagnosis, pricing, scheduling and service.

Can it guarantee more booked jobs?

No. LeadPilot is designed to improve response and lead capture, but results depend on traffic, demand, sales follow-up and other factors.

Can the AI diagnose HVAC problems?

It should not present itself as a licensed technician or make definitive technical diagnoses. Its job is to gather information and route the inquiry appropriately.

Why start with a 30-day pilot?

A pilot lets you measure captured and qualified inquiries before deciding whether LeadPilot is a good long-term fit.
